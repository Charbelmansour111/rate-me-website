## Flags

- **Hosting: Vercel's Hobby plan is for non-commercial, personal use only.** A business site that
  collects leads for a product is commercial use; move to the Pro plan (or another host) before
  launch, or confirm the plan the project is on.
- **GSAP**: the licence is free but not open source. It may not be used in a product that competes
  with Webflow's own visual animation tools; this site is not one.
- **React Bits**: the Commons Clause forbids reselling the components, alone or as a pack. Using
  them inside the site is fine.
- **The walk's 3D scene**: our renders, but every bought or downloaded asset inside the scene
  carries its own licence.
